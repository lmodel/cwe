# Policies

* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The rules for AI-assisted work here: whoever submits owns the contribution, an element says what the XSD says, a rule states only what the upstream states, a mapping is read at its source, and no commit carries an AI co-authorship trailer.
* [Licence and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - Whose work each part is: the code and the schema under Apache-2.0, the bundle under CC BY 4.0, the CWE List and its XSD under MITRE's CWE Terms of Use, with the designation and licence those terms ask each copy to reproduce. No endorsement by MITRE or the CWE Program is implied.
* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability in this repository, by GitHub's private reporting, what executes here and what holds each surface. A problem in the CWE List or its XSD belongs with the CWE Program.
